Pop out everywhere

Recommended

A card button opens its card as a window beside the button, over the view. Main still shows or hides the whole stack. Tap the button again, or ✕, to put it away.

Dock in the column everywhere

Today's rail, on every bar

A card button swaps the right column to just that card, wherever the button is. Main swaps the column back to the whole stack. Tap the lit button to put the column away.

How they differ

  • Pop out keeps the stack where it is and lays one card over it, next to your thumb. It matches Affinity, and what you picked for the docks redesign. A Brush button on the left opens the Brush card on the left.
  • Dock keeps the view clear, because every card lands in the same right-hand column. But the column stops showing the stack, and a button on the left dock opens its card on the far side of the screen.
  • Both keep Main as the button for the whole stack. In both, cards are grouped in the stack only, since the rail's own groups are already gone (C1).
