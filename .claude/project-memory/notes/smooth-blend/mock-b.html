B · Pick on the view

WITH lists the joined objects and offers Pick on the view…: the card stays open, a bar asks to tap objects, each pickable object gets a ring (accent when joined, dashed when not) and the fillets' seams show as dashed lines while picking.

  • Two lines for Bolt: touch first, you point at what to join where you see it; good for crowded figures with unnamed parts.
  • Where it is weak: a mode (Done to leave), and a second gesture to learn; the list of joined objects is still needed to remove one.
