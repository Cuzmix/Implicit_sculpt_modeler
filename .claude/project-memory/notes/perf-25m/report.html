Sculpting at 25M polygons

Analysis for Bolt · 31 Oct 2026 · measured at odin-sculpt 191436b on the cloud VM (4 threads, release builds, SwiftShader as the only GPU). Nothing in the repository was changed.

The short answer

The stroke itself is not where 25M hurts most. A large Draw or Clay frame costs 2.8 ms of CPU at 25M triangles and 5.2 ms at 25M quads here, inside a 120 Hz frame. What hurts is Smooth (14.4 ms a frame at 25M quads), the first ten seconds or so after a Remesh (12 s to remesh, 5 s to get ready, then 8.7 s of simplified copies built 4 ms at a time on the frame thread), both tablets culling on the CPU, and memory on the iPad.

Moving the brush, the raycast or the uploads to the GPU would not pay: they cost 0.02 ms (raycast) and 1 to 3 MB a frame (uploads), and the brush would lose exact undo and the CPU-equals-parallel rule. The GPU work worth doing is culling on Metal and on the Mali tablet, and the simplified pass while orbiting. The rest of the speed comes from CPU work done better.

What "25M polygons when remeshed" means here

Remesh makes quads by default, and a quad remesh has about one vertex per quad. So 25M polygons is about 25M vertices and 50M triangles to sculpt on. If Quads was off it is 12.5M vertices and 25M triangles. Every table below has both.

Two things the profile shows

  • Normals are a third of every frame. A flush recomputes every vertex of every node a dab touched, even the far side of a node the brush only grazed, and a neighbour node's border test looks each triangle's node up through two arrays. Normals scale well (2.8× on 4 threads); they are simply a lot of work.
  • A dab scales poorly across cores. At 25M triangles a large dab takes 0.87 ms on 1 thread, 0.70 on 2 and 0.45 on 4: 1.9× on 4 threads. Each dab is 3 to 4 parallel phases (area, undo copy, move, bounds), each with a barrier, over about a hundred nodes. On the 12-thread PC and the 8-core tablets the gap grows. The app's own run agrees: brush_ms p50 0.95, normals_ms p50 0.71, cpu_ms p50 2.0 at 25M triangles.

Proposed plan, ranked by what you will feel

Estimates are mine and unmeasured until each step is built; every step ends with the gate, a bench A/B (alternating runs, ranges) and the numbers in its PR.

  1. A 25M bench row and a stroke profiler in the repo

    The harness I used here (loops real strokes, undoes each, prints per-phase times) becomes bench --profile-stroke, with 12.5M and 25M rows, so every later step is measured the same way instead of from scratch patches.

    ~150 linesbench only

  2. Simplified copies built off the frame thread, and the close-view fix

    The copies are built on a worker that stops when a stroke starts (the autosave thread's pattern), so idle frames after a Remesh drop from about 6.4 ms back to about 1.5 ms and orbiting gets simplified as fast as the cores allow. Fix the selection that draws 2.3× more triangles close up at 50M.

    ~300 linesneeds a design note: threads and the sculptorest. −4 to −5 ms on ~2,000 frames after a 25M Remesh

  3. Normals: only what moved, and a cheaper border test

    Recompute a vertex's normal only where a triangle round it moved (the move pass marks them), and find a triangle's node from the node ranges instead of two lookups. Normals move to their own file out of the 2,047-line brush.odin.

    ~250 linesest. normals 1.5–2× faster: Draw frame at 25M quads 5.2 → about 4.2 ms

  4. Smooth twice as fast

    Smooth reads every corner of every triangle round a vertex (18 reads for 6 neighbours) on each pass. A neighbour list per node, made on first touch in a stroke and freed at its end, cuts that to 6 and keeps memory to what the stroke reached. Results stay deterministic but round differently from today's.

    ~250 linesest. Smooth frame 14.4 → about 7 ms at 25M quads

  5. Fewer barriers per dab

    The undo copy and the bounds join the move pass, so a dab is two parallel phases instead of four. Exact same results. Matters most on the PC's 12 threads and the tablets' 8 cores.

    ~150 linesest. 10–20% off a dab

  6. GPU culling on Metal and on Mali, and for the simplified pass

    The cull kernels get Metal sources and zero-count commands; the Mali path is checked against lazy memory; a third kernel picks simplified copies. Pixels stay identical (parity_check at 0 px).

    ~450 lines, a shaderneeds a design noteyou check on the iPad and the OnePlus Pad

  7. Undo without the hitch

    Undo and redo wait for the normal cones as a stroke's end does, so the 34 ms at 25M quads drops to about half.

    ~60 linestouches undo: short design note

  8. Memory diet for the iPad

    Drop the BVH's triangle order once the mesh is renumbered (it is the identity: 200 MB at 50M triangles) and keep CPU normals packed to 4 bytes (200 MB at 25M vertices). About 400 MB of the 2.2 GB of mesh data at 25M quads.

    ~200 linesdynamic topology and levels checked with it

  9. Faster Remesh and ready-to-sculpt

    Keep and reuse the remesher's large buffers, ask Linux and Android for huge pages on them, and start the upload while the sculptor is being built. Then, with your PC's numbers, decide on a GPU Remesh.

    ~200 linesremesh gains need a device run to size

Put together, my estimate for 25M quads on this VM is a Draw frame of about 3.5 ms (from 5.2), a Smooth frame of about 7 ms (from 14.4), and orbiting simplified within a few seconds of a Remesh instead of after eight to nine seconds of slow frames.

Cleanup and structure found on the way

  • The GPU seam still speaks SDL_GPU's type names. SDL_GPU is retired, but render names its own buffers, passes and pipelines as sdl.GPU… in 1,018 places over 35 files. Render's own handle types would make the seam honest. Large but mechanical; best done when no other thread is in render.
  • brush.odin is 2,047 lines. The dab pipeline, normals and Smooth each deserve a file; steps 3 and 4 above do that as they go.
  • The interface costs about 1 ms a frame (ui_ms p50 0.9 to 1.2, three quarters of an idle frame at 100k). Worth a look of its own; not mesh-related.
  • Why a capability is off is not said anywhere. GPU culling is off on Metal and under lazy memory inside one line of hal.odin; the exit stats should name the reason, as they do for ray query.

What I could not measure, and what would settle it

  • Any GPU time: this VM has only SwiftShader. On the PC, build.bat gpubench --vertices=12500000 and then --vertices=25000000 would show whether the RTX stays under 1 ms at 25M quads and how long orbiting runs at full detail before the copies are ready.
  • The iPad's memory ceiling at 25M quads, and its stroke times on 4 performance cores: python scripts/try_it.py after a Remesh to 25M would answer both.
  • How much of Remesh's 12 s is the VM's slow first touch of memory: the PC's Remesh time from the result line.
