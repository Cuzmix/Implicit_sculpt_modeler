Same in every direction

  • The Material card is the one material UI; MatCaps move to the Shading menu's Clay look; the Look card becomes Light (Lights, Environment, Background, Floor, Viewport style).
  • Balls are rendered by our viewport PBR path into an atlas (≈ 6.5 MB for 64), at most 4 a still frame.
  • Imported glTF / FBX / USD materials are kept per object (one per group), not baked into paint (render worker's E8).
  • Layers as folds: Surface, Subsurface, Coat, Sheen, Transmission, Glow (Thin film under More).
  • Render card: Off | Fast | Full, a progress line, Draft | Good | Final, Output and Passes folds, then Lens, Finish, Turntable, 3D print.
