Materials · C · Studio

C · Studio. Toolbag's materials panel as a wide card: the scene's materials as a strip on top, categories and the grid on the left, the editor always open on the right. The Render switch leaves the card for a pill over the view (Off | Fast | Full, progress, Save image); the Render card keeps the settings.

The balls here are CSS stand-ins. In the app each one is drawn by our own renderer (the viewport's PBR path under the scene's environment) into a ball atlas. Lens theme tokens; card 312 pt. All directions · design note: design.md

Trade-offs

  • Best on desktop: library, scene materials and values all visible; no mode switch between browse and edit.
  • A new card shape (own-body, as Colours and History), and over half a tablet's view; on a phone-sized window it would fall back to A.
  • The render pill is a second place for the Render switch unless the card loses it, which this direction does: the card holds settings only.
