Rail groups, one Scene card and fuller lights

Your seven asks, checked against the code on odin-sculpt. Three small ones are built and merged today. The rail groups, the Scene merge and the new light types are planned below with live mocks, and wait for your go.

Where I'd push back

A few of these aren't quite what the code shows. None of it changes what you want, but it changes what we build.

You: panels are hard-coded and can't be added
Any panel can go on the rail today, but only through Arrange, then +, then typing its name in Search. Nobody will find that.
Fix: the rail's + opens a Panels picker, a grid of every panel by icon, always one tap away.
You: groups would be new
The Panels stack already joins cards into tab groups (drag a card's header onto another). The rail just doesn't use that model.
Fix: one group model for both. A rail button holds one panel or a group of up to six; the stack and the rail read the same thing.
You: Scene and Objects do the same thing
Mostly, but not completely. The Objects card has the picture rows and the Actions (Split, Merge, Transpose, Boolean, Align, Distribute, Project) that Scene lacks. Deleting it would lose those.
Fix: Scene takes both. A Rows switch (Compact or Pictures) and the Actions folded at its foot. The Objects card goes.
You: lights need colour and more settings
Colour, colour temperature, size (sun angle, lamp radius), spot cone, soft edge, distance and "moves with" already exist. They're folded under More, so the popover shows only Intensity.
Fix: show them. Then add what's actually missing: area lights, per-light shadows, highlight strength, aim at the selection, presets.
You: raise the shelf cap
On the iPad, 12 tiles is all that fits before your palm (8 full tiles' width, narrowed to 42 points). More per page only shows on a desktop, or if the shelf may run under the palm.
Built: 24 per page and on the rail, plus a switch to let the shelf use the full width on a tablet. Pages (up to 12 of them) are still the iPad's real answer.
You: the gizmo moved by accident
It moved on purpose in #472 (U2, one View island), which you picked. Top right is still the better home: the pen reaches it, and ZBrush, Blender and Nomad all put it there.
Built: top right is the default again, with a setting to keep it in the View island.

Rail groups approved

Try it: drag Camera onto Layers to make a group. Tap a button to open its card; a group opens as tabs, or switch it to Stacked in the card's header to see every panel at once, each folding on its own. Drag a tab off the card (or tap its ×) to split it out. Right-click or hold a rail button or a tab for its settings: name, colour, tabs or stacked, pin it open in the panel column, ungroup. The + at the foot opens the Panels picker.

5 buttons, no groups
Dragging a button onto another joins them; between two moves it. Right-click or hold a group (or a tab) for its settings.

How it behaves in the app

  • Join: hold a rail button for the hold delay, then drag it onto another (or drag freely in Arrange). The target lights; let go and it becomes a group button: the two icons stacked with a count. Dropping between buttons moves it instead, as now.
  • Open: a tap opens the card beside the rail as today. A group shows as tabs (one panel at a time, remembering the last) or stacked (every panel open down one scrolling card, each header folding its panel, like the Panels stack and Blender's sidebar). Tabs or Stacked sits in the card's header and is kept per group. A second tap on the rail button closes it.
  • Group settings: a long press (the hold delay) on a group's rail button or on any of its tabs, or a right-click, opens its popover: Name (typed, or the panels' names), Colour (a strip on the rail button and a dot on the card, none by default), Show as Tabs or Stacked, Pin open in the panel column, Ungroup, Remove from rail. A plain panel gets the same popover without Show as and Ungroup.
  • One model, no second stack. The rail is the panel stack folded to icons, as Photoshop's dock collapses to icons. A tap pops a group out beside the rail and a press on the view puts it away; a pinned group docks in the column instead, pinned groups stacking down it in rail order. The column keeps the stack's Left, Right and Float; the separate Panels button, the stack's own card order and its second column ("beside") retire. A saved layout's stack lines become pinned rail groups once, on load.
  • Split: drag a tab off the card back onto the rail, or use × on the tab. A group of one becomes a plain button again.
  • Add: the + at the rail's foot is always there (not only in Arrange). It opens the Panels picker; a panel already on the rail shows as such, and dropping a picker tile onto a rail button groups it straight away.
  • Every panel is a panel. Camera, Shading and View layout are popover menus today; each gets its card (the Camera section already exists), so they can be grouped like the rest.
  • Saved per workspace as rail lines (rail_group 0 Layers Camera), up to 6 panels a group. Older builds read the first panel of each group and skip the rest.

  • Area lights in the viewport use the nearest-point trick we already use for sized lamps, extended to a rectangle or disk: soft, broad highlights at almost no extra cost.
