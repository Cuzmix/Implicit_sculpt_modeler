Bézier curves: editable strokes, stamps, tubes and Model mode

Round 2, after your review: your four defaults are now decisions, and the plan adds Nomad 2.9's hold-to-edit strokes, stamps you can edit before validating, and curves in Model mode (states 6 to 8 in the mock). This is a plan for editable curves after Nomad's Tube, Lathe and editable strokes, ZBrush's curve brushes and Blender's curve objects. Today a Curve drag makes a tube, stitches or a zipper the moment you lift, and it can never be edited again. With this plan a curve stays a live object with control points, handles and per-point radius until you press Bake. Nothing is built yet; this page is for your review.

The mock

Pick a state. In the viewport you can drag the blue control points, the orange handles and the white radius dots. Double-click a point to switch it between smooth and corner. The cards and bars use the app's own Graphite theme and the three-island top bar.

What will exist

One curve object, four uses new

A curve is its own kind of object in the scene list, with a curve icon. It holds cubic Bézier control points, each with two handles, a radius, a twist and a smooth or corner type. What it makes is a setting you can change at any time: Tube, Lathe, Along (copies along it) or Brush path. The mesh it makes is rebuilt live while you edit, in a job off the frame.

Hold to edit a stroke new

As in Nomad 2.9: end any stroke and keep the pen still for about half a second. The stroke is fitted to a few points and stays live. Radius and intensity are each One or Each, so a stroke can start faint, swell and fade out. Tap a point to make it square, tap the curve to add one, drag a point onto its neighbour to merge, and Smoothness sets how many points the fit keeps. Every edit re-runs the stroke from the surface as it was before it, so the result is exactly what a perfect hand would have drawn. Validate makes it one undo step on the active layer, so layer strength can fade it later. Works for sculpt brushes, paint and the mask.

Edit a stamp before it lands new

After a Rectangle or Drag dot stamp, the stamp stays live: drag its middle to slide it over the surface, the dot on the notched ring to turn it (it clicks to every 15° and the long notch marks 0°), the corner to size it and the green dot for intensity. Pressing elsewhere validates it and starts the next, so a row of stamps stays quick.

Curves in Model mode new

  • Pipe: a quad tube cage, with a ring at each control point, segments between points and creased corner points, ready for Catmull-Clark levels.
  • Lathe as a quad cage, and Loft between two or more curves.
  • Spine, Extrude and Knife can follow a curve instead of a drawn path, for clean hard-surface shapes.
  • Edge loop to curve: pick a loop on a cage and get a curve from it, like ZBrush's Frame mesh.

Drawing and editing

  • Drag to draw freehand. On lift the stroke is fitted to as few Bézier points as keep it within a tolerance, so you get 5 points, not 200.
  • Tap, tap, tap places points one by one (Nomad's path). Drag while placing to pull out handles (Illustrator and Blender's pen).
  • Points stick to the surface with an offset, or to the view plane through the first hit, or to a grid axis.
  • Tap the curve to add a point there; drag a point off the curve and back to delete it, or use the context bar.

Shape of a tube

  • Radius One, Ends or Each, as Nomad cycles it, plus a taper graph along the length (Blender's taper object, ZBrush's curve profile).
  • Profile presets: round, square, ribbon, star, half round; or draw your own closed shape. Per-point profiles blend between points.
  • Twist per point, caps flat, round or open, closed loop, sides and lengthwise density, mirror X live.

What happens to today's Curve tool exists

Tube, Stitches and Zipper in curve_tool.odin become presets of the new curve: Tube is Tube with a round profile, Stitches and Zipper are Along with the built-in stitch and tooth parts. Their sweep and placement code in geom/curve_mesh.odin is reused, and the rotation-minimising frames get stronger for long curves. Lazy, Line and Join on brushes stay as they are; Brush path adds the precise version.

Build plan

Thirteen pull requests into odin-sculpt, each through the quick gate and the full gate at the end. The editable stroke and stamp come first because they help every brush. PR 2 and PR 4 each get a design note for the architect review first: PR 2 changes how a stroke is undone, and PR 4 adds a scene object kind, a file chunk and undo entries.

  1. Bézier curves in geom

    Cubic segments, length-even sampling, rotation-minimising frames with per-point twist, nearest point on a curve, and fitting a freehand stroke to few points within a tolerance. Tests for serial against parallel, closed loops and degenerate handles.

    src/geom/bezier.odin · tests

  2. Hold to edit a stroke

    The stroke keeps its samples and the nodes it touched. A still pen at the end opens editing: the fit, per-point radius and intensity, square points, merge, add, Smoothness. Each edit restores the touched nodes and re-runs the stroke through apply_dab, mask_dab or paint, throttled so the pen stays smooth. Validate is one undo step on the active layer.

    sculpt_tool.odin, stroke record, overlay · touch scenarios for the hold

  3. Edit a stamp

    Rectangle and Drag dot stamps stay live until validated: slide over the surface, turn on the notched ring with snaps, size and intensity handles, re-run from the saved nodes on each change.

  4. The curve object

    A new object kind beside meshes, voxels and cages, in the scene list, saved in a new CURV chunk that older apps step over, with undo entries for point edits. Draw by drag or by taps, on the surface, a view plane or an axis.

    src/app/curve_object.odin · files.odin chunk · History

  5. Editing on the pen

    Points, handles and radius dots in the overlay, rope pull between points, add by tap, smooth or corner, delete, merge, mirror X, and the context bar. The stroke editor of PR 2 shares this code.

  6. Tube

    Radius One, Ends and Each, the taper graph, profile presets and a drawn profile, per-point profiles, twist, caps, sides and density. Rebuilt in a job while editing; Bake makes a sculptable mesh. Today's Tube becomes this.

  7. Lathe

    The curve spun round the view's vertical, world X, Y or Z, or the selected object's axis, with an angle and segment count.

  8. Along

    Stitches, zipper teeth, a primitive or any scene object placed along the curve. Today's Stitches and Zipper become presets of this.

  9. Brush path

    Apply runs the current brush, mask or paint along a curve object, as one stroke and one undo step, repeatable.

  10. Pipe and Lathe cages in Model mode

    Quad cages from a curve in src/poly: rings at control points, segments between, creases at corner points, caps, then the usual Catmull-Clark levels and Apply to sculpt.

    src/poly · model_tool.odin

  11. Spine, Extrude and Knife along a curve, Edge loop to curve

    The existing Spine and Knife take a curve in place of a drawn path; Extrude gets a Follow curve choice; a picked edge loop becomes a curve object.

  12. Loft

    A quad cage bridged across two or more curves, matched by length, with a count of rings between them.

  13. Help, README and the full gate

    Help pages, README controls, screenshots of each use, and the full gate with touch scenarios.

Decided, and new questions with defaults

Decided in round 1Curves stay live until Bake. A drag draws freehand and taps place points. A baked tube is its own object. Model mode curves are now part of the plan.
Should a mouse get hold to edit too?Default: yes, holding the button still at the end works the same. A setting can make every stroke open for editing, off by default.
Should a held stroke be editable on dynamic-topology objects or voxel clay?Default: no, as those strokes change the triangles; the hold does nothing there and the stroke stays as drawn.
Should Pipe, Lathe and Loft make a new cage, or join the selected cage?Default: a new cage object; Weld and Bridge join it, as with other parts.
