One split view for every kind of pane

Instead of the shader graph and the UV editor each bringing their own overlay, split and full modes, the split view the app already has learns to hold them. Every pane gets a kind (3D view, UVs, Graph, Stack, Texture) and a place (docked in the split, floating see-through over the others, full, or a sheet on a phone). The mock below runs: the egg, the unwrap, the paint and the graph all work.

What the enhanced split view adds

1 · Pane kinds

3D view, UVs, Graph, Stack, Texture now; Render preview, Reference picture and Timeline later. A kind that only one pane can show (the UV pane, the graph) moves when picked elsewhere.

2 · Four places

Docked, Floating see-through (the three.js idea, for every kind), Full with chips for the others, Sheet on a phone. A pane keeps its kind as it moves between them.

3 · The shelf follows the pane

Touch the UV pane and the shelf shows Island, Relax, Pack; touch the graph and it shows Add node, Tidy, Probe. One shelf, never three toolbars.

4 · Panes that talk

A seam drawn in 3D shows in the UV pane, a picked island lights up in 3D, paint on the Texture pane shows on the model, Probe on the model lights up every node.

5 · Portrait turns

A side by side becomes stacked when the window is taller than wide, so an iPad turned to portrait keeps both panes usable; turning back restores it.

6 · Phone sheet

The first pane fills the screen and the others are tabs in a sheet with two heights, so a phone gets the same layouts without a second design.

Plan: rebuild the split view around pane kinds

This comes before the Surface work (UV, Paint, Shade): those stages then add a kind each instead of building their own windows.

V1

Pane kinds. Each pane holds a kind with its own state (today's view pane state becomes the 3D kind's); the kind chip in the pane's head; the kinds that only one pane can show move when picked elsewhere; workspaces keep a pane's kind on a new line that older apps skip. Ships with 3D only, so nothing changes yet.

V2

Floating panes. Any pane floats over the others (up to two at once), dragged by its head, resized by its corner, with See-through and Hold to peek; Dock puts it back beside the pane it floated from. Full gains the chips of the other panes.

V3

The shelf follows the pane. The pane last touched picks the shelf's page, as the space picks it today; focus shown by the pane's outline; keys go to the pane under the pointer.

V4

Portrait and phone. Side by side turns stacked when the window is taller than wide (a setting, on by default); under about 600 points wide the first pane fills and the others become sheet tabs with two heights.

V5

First new kinds. Texture (an object's maps, paintable) and Reference picture, which need no new engine and prove the kinds out. Then S1 onward adds UVs, then Stack and Graph.

Choices for Bolt

Build the split view first? Recommended: yes, V1 to V4 before the Surface stages, so UVs and the graph arrive as kinds. The other way: build Surface's panes on their own now and merge them into the split view later, which repeats work.

How many panes. Recommended: four docked plus two floating, as the tree holds four today. More than four docked is cramped on a tablet.

Floating see-through for 3D views too? Recommended: yes but drawn at rest, as view panes are, so a floating camera over the model costs nothing while you sculpt.
