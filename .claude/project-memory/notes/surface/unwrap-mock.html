Same for all three: Unwrap runs as a long job with progress and Cancel, and makes one undo step (a new History kind that holds the old and new UVs). A 100k-triangle sphere takes about 1.8 s on this cloud VM, faster on the iPad. On touch the rows use the touch density, and on a phone the card is a sheet.
