Docks and Cards

Two kinds of thing, and nothing else. A dock holds items in rows or columns on an edge or floating. A card is one piece of the interface, the same card wherever it opens. Use the app below, or switch to Arrange and pick any dock to change it.

The hard rules

  1. Two nouns. Everything on screen is a dock or a card. Inside a dock there are only items: a button, a value or a card.
  2. One dock type. The rail, the shelf, the island, the top bar's islands, the view chips, the dial and your own docks are all Dock. They differ only in their data: place, direction, lines, size, labels, pages.
  3. One card per thing. Each card has one id, one title, one icon and one body. Every way in (a dock, Search, a menu, a shortcut) shows that card. A second interface for the same thing is a bug.
  4. A card shows two ways. As an icon, a tap opens it beside the dock, and a second tap or a tap outside closes it. As a card, it stands open in the dock. A dock picks one for its cards, and any card can change.
  5. Tabs are a card item holding several cards. No separate group type: drop a card on a card to make tabs, drag a tab out to split.
  6. One way to save. A dock is one line in the layout: dock <name> <place> <direction> <lines> ... and an item line for each item. Old files are read once and turned into docks.
  7. Banned words in code. panel, palette, section, tray, rail, bar, shelf, island, stack, column (as a container). They may stay as names a person gives a dock, never as types.
