Docks and Cards

v2

Affinity's model: a dock button pops out a window, a window holds cards as tabs, and you pin, move, resize, join and tear off. Everything on the stage is live.

Things to try

  1. Tap a button in the right dock: its window pops out beside the dock. Tap the view and it goes away.
  2. Pin it with the pin, or just drag its bar or resize its corner: a moved window stays.
  3. Look is a group: one window with three tabs.
  4. Drag a tab out of a window: it tears off into its own pinned window.
  5. Drag a tab onto another window: it joins it as a tab.
  6. Drag a dock button onto a window to add its card there, or onto the view to open it pinned where you drop it.
  7. Tap a dock's ⋯: every dock gets outlined and the card sheet opens. Drag a card onto a dock, drag buttons to reorder or off to remove, and set columns with the slider.
  8. In Dock settings, change a dock's edge or its number of columns.

Words we use

Hard rules

    Dock settings

    Every dock is the same kind of thing. Only these settings differ.

    How the layout is saved

    One dock line per dock, a button line per button, a window line per pinned window. Unpinned windows are not saved.
