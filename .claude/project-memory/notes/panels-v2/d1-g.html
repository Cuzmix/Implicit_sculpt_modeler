D2: any edge

Playground D1 next

Every dock carries a faint grip at its start and a faint corner on the side facing the middle. They are there all the time, not only in Edit docks.

  • drag gripCarry the dock. Edge strips light up. Drop near an edge to dock there, or anywhere else to float on the soft grid.
  • tap gripThe dock's menu: lines, names, Edit buttons, Reset place.
  • drag cornerAway from the edge for more lines of buttons (1 to 3), back for fewer.
  • D1 vs D2After D1, columns take the left and right edges or float, and the shelf stays at the bottom. After D2, a top or bottom edge turns any dock into a row, and the shelf moves too.

Two docks on one edge swap places when one is dropped on the other, as Arrange does today.
