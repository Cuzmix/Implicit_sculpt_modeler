Docks and Cards v7.2 proposal

Two kinds only. Docks hold buttons and move like Affinity's: on an edge or floating, with a faint corner to resize. Twelve cards hold the controls and open in the column beside the dock you tapped. Tap opens, Pin keeps, drag the head out to float. Try it as an iPad, a narrow window or a Windows desktop.

Try this

  1. Tap Tool on the pen-side dock. It opens beside the dock.
  2. Tap the model. The card closes and the tap does not sculpt.
  3. Pin Tool, then open Layers. On a tablet Layers shows in its place and Tool returns when it closes; on Windows the column splits.
  4. Drag on the model with a card pinned. Cards fade under the stroke.
  5. Drag a card's title into the view. It floats. Drop it on a dock to put it back.
  6. Search for "remesh" and pick it. The Mesh card opens on that control.
  7. Switch to Narrow. A card becomes a bottom sheet.
  8. Switch to Windows. Compact rows, names beside icons, hover tips.
  9. Drag any dock, the shelf too, by its faint grip: put it anywhere and it floats; near any edge it sits there, and on the top or bottom edge it becomes a row. Floating docks line up with each other's edges, as they do today. Pull its faint corner to add lines.
  10. Hold ⋯ on a dock (or click it) to edit its buttons; + opens the Library.

  • Pop out versus Keep open
  • Side panels, the Stats and Objects windows
