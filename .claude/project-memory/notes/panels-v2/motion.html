Panels motion

Eight places where the panels still snap. Press Play on each one to see it move. Today shows how the app behaves now. Proposed shows the new motion. Every motion gets its own switch under Settings > Interface > Motion, follows Reduced and Off, and never runs during a stroke or a camera drag.

Speed

1Cards slide from their dock start here

The card comes out from behind the dock that opened it, so you can see where it came from and where it goes back to. Opening another card in the same column slides the old one away. Closing slides it back in. Today a card fades and rises 12 points when it opens, and simply disappears when it closes.

Time
220 ms open, 180 ms close, eased out
Switch
Cards (new)
Reduced
a fade only

2Bottom sheet on narrow screens start here

Below 900 points a card opens as a sheet over the lower part. It rises from the bottom edge. Dragging its grip down makes it follow your finger, and letting go past a third of its height puts it away; letting go sooner springs it back. Today it fades in, and only its close button puts it away.

Time
260 ms rise, follows the finger exactly, 200 ms away
Switch
Cards
Reduced
a fade; the drag still works

3Sheets fade and grow in start here

Settings, Help, Library, Export and Macros open by growing slightly from 96% while the view dims behind them, and leave with a quicker fade. This is the iPadOS form sheet's feel. Today they appear and vanish in one frame.

200 ms in, 150 ms out

Reduced

4Docks slide off their edge start here

Focus mode, the clean view and Reset layout send each dock off the edge it stands on and bring it back the same way, a few milliseconds apart, so the screen clears without a jolt. A floating dock fades instead. Today they pop out and in.

Time
240 ms, 30 ms apart
Switch

Reduced

5Dropped docks glide into place

A dock let go near an edge glides the last stretch to where it snaps, rather than jumping there. In Edit buttons, a button you add opens a gap and grows into it, and one you remove shrinks while its neighbours close up.

200 ms glide, 180 ms per button

Docks
Reduced

6Floating cards lift and return

Tearing a card out of its column lifts it slightly, a little larger with a deeper shadow, so it reads as picked up. Put back glides it into its slot in the column. Today it doesn't lift, and Put back moves it in one frame.

120 ms lift, 260 ms return
Switch
Cards
Reduced

7Second view slides in, panes grow

Second view brings the divider in from the edge and the pane opens behind it; a second tap takes it back out. Maximise grows a pane from its own rectangle to the whole view, and Restore shrinks it back. Today the pane fades in and Maximise jumps. The 3D views keep drawing at rest only, so this costs no extra renders.

Time
260 ms split, 240 ms maximise
Switch
View panes (exists, today only the fade)
Reduced
the fade it has now

8Switching space fades across

Going from Sculpt to Render, the docks and cards that leave fade out quickly, and the new space's ones fade in and settle a few points. A card the new layout pins, such as Render's Look card, slides out from its dock as in 1. Only the tab indicator moves today; everything else changes in one frame.

Time
120 ms out, 180 ms in
Switch
Spaces (new)
Reduced
the fade without the settle

How it would be built: every motion uses the kit's existing tweens (ui.presence, ui.tween, transform_since), which already respect the Motion level. A closing card is drawn for its last 180 ms from what it showed, so a panel still keeps no state between frames. Cost: the interface's vertices move on the CPU only while something animates, the 3D view is not redrawn for any of it, and nothing animates while a brush or the camera is held. Suggested order: one PR for 1 to 4, then one for 5 to 8.
