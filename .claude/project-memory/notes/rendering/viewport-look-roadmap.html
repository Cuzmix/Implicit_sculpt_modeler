Sculpt · rendering audit and plan · 27 Oct 2026

Viewport look, rendering and PBR: what to add next

The renderer is fast and structurally sound. What holds the picture back is the colour pipeline and the lighting around the materials, not light transport. The plan fixes those first with zero-cost shader work, then adds MatCaps and anti-aliasing, then per-object materials and material export. Path tracing waits.

Built from a code audit of the odin-sculpt branch, a baseline run, and three independent research threads (viewport shading, advanced rendering, PBR and baking). Full reports are in notes/rendering/ in the project files.

Where things stand

What works, what needs work, what is missing

Keep as is
  • Partial redraw, idle-object cache, per-chunk culling and LOD (why a 10M stroke is ~0.4 ms GPU on the RTX)
  • Classic clay shading, flat shading, wireframe, mask, polypaint, polygroups, density checker
  • Studio render (Shift+R): progressive AO and soft key shadow
  • GGX maths in the PBR path (checked line by line: correct)
  • GPU compute trial, CPU BVH, normal and displacement bake
Needs improvement
  • Gamma 2.2 instead of the sRGB curve: crevices come out grey (linear 0.001 shows as 11/255, not 3/255)
  • Tone mapping on PBR only; no exposure
  • PBR environment: grey and symmetric, key light counted twice, no specular anti-aliasing or horizon occlusion
  • One global material; unselected objects always grey; not saved in the scene
  • Colour picker means linear for materials and sRGB for paint
Missing
  • Anti-aliasing of any kind
  • Cavity or curvature shading in the viewport
  • MatCaps (no texture is bound to the mesh shader)
  • Editable or world-locked lights, HDRI lighting, background gradient, floor
  • Materials in glTF/USD export; AO, ID, colour, curvature bakes; textured preview

Research verdicts

What the three research threads concluded

Viewport shading

The look sculptors want is MatCap plus cavity, and both are a texture fetch and a few derivative instructions with no new pass, so partial redraw and the idle cache are untouched. Fix the colour pipeline first. MSAA is the only essential item with a real GPU cost (estimated 10M stroke from 0.41 to about 0.7 ms at 1080p). No TAA or FXAA: they break deterministic dumps or blur stroke detail.

Advanced rendering

Not now. SDL_GPU 3.4.18 has compute, storage buffers and indirect dispatch, so a compute ray tracer is feasible, but it exposes no ray-tracing hardware and no Vulkan handles; a native RT side path would mean forking SDL. A path tracer with one global material and no HDRI would render a grey ball in a box. Next after the raster work: compute-traced AO and shadows inside the existing Studio render.

PBR, texturing, baking

Keep the material record glTF-shaped with OpenPBR names; map to MaterialX/OpenPBR on export only. Polypaint stays the texturing primary at 10M+ vertices; no texture painting in the sculpt. Next milestone: colour-space fix, per-object materials saved in the scene, materials in glTF/USD, then a bake set (colour, ID, AO, curvature, thickness) and GLB with textures.

The plan

Roadmap, in order

Each item ships on its own and is checked with --dump-viewport PPM diffs, the gate (tests, smoke under GPU validation) and gpubench at 500k to 10M. Every new control is a registry command with a script verb. Tags say whether an item improves existing code, extends a system, adds a missing capability, or is an experiment.

Stage 1 · Viewport look

high value, mostly zero GPU cost
1.1
Building nowImprovementeffort S · ~0 GPU cost
Colour pipeline, cavity shading and specular fixes

Exact sRGB encode in mesh.frag and decode of polypaint in mesh.vert; one tone mapper (Khronos PBR Neutral) on both the classic and PBR paths; an Exposure setting; Cavity and Ridge shading from the screen-space change of the normal, with two sliders; specular anti-aliasing and horizon occlusion on PBR materials. All of it is per-pixel in the existing shader, so partial redraw and the idle cache keep working once the new settings are part of their keys.

Reuses: Material_Uniforms, the Material section in the Scene palette, the settings table, frame_key/static_key. Accept: crevice pixels darker by the expected amount, same flags give 0-pixel diffs, Cavity 0 and Exposure 0 keep the classic look within rounding, gate green.

1.2
Improvementeffort S · 0 cost
PBR studio rig

Stop counting the key light twice and replace the grey symmetric environment with a coloured, asymmetric three-lobe studio, so chrome and steel read on flat faces. Tuned on your RTX, not on the software renderer.

1.3
New capabilityeffort M · 1-2 texture fetches
MatCaps

The ZBrush sculpting look. A sampler on the mesh pipeline, embedded PNG matcaps uploaded with mipmaps, ZBrush's two-sphere layout so polypaint and the mask still show, and Blender's perspective-correct lookup. Shared with content packs (Phase 7.4c), which planned "materials and matcaps"; the shader design comes from here.

1.4
New capabilityeffort M/L · est. +0.3 ms at 1080p
MSAA 4x (2x and off as fallbacks)

Multisampled colour and depth resolved into the existing viewport texture. SDL cannot copy or resolve multisampled depth, so the idle cache and the Studio render need reworking: the riskiest item in the stage. Budget: 10M stroke at most 0.75 ms GPU at 1080p, with Off restoring today's pixels exactly. Default 2x or off on tablets.

1.5
Extensioneffort S, M with a light-sphere widget
Editable and world-locked lights

Key and fill as uniforms with direction, colour and strength; a World lock so a turntable shows real light. The Studio render's shadow follows the key. Later, a LightCap-style sphere and "bake lights to a matcap".

Stage 4 · Advanced rendering

postponed until Stages 1-2 land
4.1
Extensioneffort L
HDRI lighting, HDR target, idle-only GTAO

A Radiance .hdr reader, prefiltered cube map and SH irradiance; a float intermediate only once a post pass needs linear input. Try the coloured procedural studio (1.2) first.

4.2
Experimentdesign review first · ~100 MB at 10M
Compute-traced Studio render

A two-level BVH on the GPU (the sculptor's nodes as the top level, rebuilt after a stroke, never during one) replacing the shadow-map gather, removing its resolution floor on fine detail.

Experiment6-10 weeks
Progressive path-traced render mode

Only once per-object materials, HDRI and the traced BVH exist. Est. 64 samples in 1.5-4 s at 1080p on the RTX.

Not doing

Ideas set aside, and why

  • TAA, FXAA: ghosting during strokes, non-deterministic dumps, or blurred fine detail.
  • A native Vulkan ray-tracing path: SDL_GPU exposes no device handles; it would fork a pinned dependency and work only on RTX cards.
  • A G-buffer or deferred rewrite, per-frame shadow maps at 10M: doubles stroke cost for effects the Studio render already gives when idle.
  • Tangents or curvature in the vertex format: memory at 50M vertices; the shader derives both.
  • MaterialX or OpenPBR as the internal model, texture painting in the sculpt, EXR, SSR, HDR display output: large surface, little value for sculpting now.
  • DLSS or other neural rendering: excluded at your request.
