View gizmo: three styles, one place

The navigation head in the pen-side top corner becomes a view gizmo with three styles you pick in Settings > Camera or by holding the gizmo: Axes (Blender's balls), Cube (Nomad's labelled cube, with edges and corners) and Model (ZBrush's head, or any model you choose from the scene). Every style turns the view when dragged, snaps it when tapped and spins it with the pen's roll. Try them below: drag to turn, tap to snap.

The styles

Axes

Default · from Blender
  • X, Y and Z balls with letters; the minus axes as hollow rings, so the side facing you is always plain.
  • Tap a ball to look from it; tap the ball already facing you to look from the other side.
  • Smallest and quietest: drawn by the interface, nothing rendered.

Cube

from Nomad and Fusion
  • Named faces, tinted by their axis, so the view is read as words.
  • Tap a face, an edge or a corner: 26 views, the edges and corners at 45°, the part under the pointer lit first.
  • A small X Y Z triad at its corner (switchable).

Model

from ZBrush
  • The head that turns with the view, as now, or your own model: Use selected object takes a light copy of it.
  • Tap a side to look from it, nearest axis wins.
  • Drawn once per turn of the view into its own small picture, as the head is today.
